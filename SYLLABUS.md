## 2. Subject 1: Computer Knowledge (90 Questions – 60%)

### A. Computer Fundamentals and Hardware (13 Questions)
- **Computer Basics:** Definition, IPO cycle, analog/digital/hybrid, micro/mini/mainframe/supercomputers, 5 generations of computers.
- **CPU & Architecture:** ALU, CU, Registers, Machine Cycle (Fetch, Decode, Execute), clock speed, 32-bit vs 64-bit, cores & threads.
- **Memory & Storage:** RAM (SRAM, DRAM), ROM (PROM, EPROM, EEPROM), Cache L1/L2/L3, HDD, SSD, optical disks, storage units (Bit to TB).
- **Input & Output Devices:** Keyboard, mouse, scanner, OCR, OMR, MICR, barcode/QR readers, monitors, impact/non-impact printers, plotters.

### B. Number System and Data Representation (7 Questions)
- **Number Systems:** Decimal, binary, octal, hexadecimal bases & interconversions.
- **Binary Arithmetic:** Addition, subtraction, 1's & 2's complement, signed & unsigned representations.
- **Data Representation:** Bit, byte, word, ASCII, EBCDIC, Unicode, UTF-8, BCD.

### C. Data and Information Concepts (1 Question)
- Data vs Information vs Database, processing cycle, accuracy, relevance, completeness, timeliness, structured/unstructured data.

### D. Operating System (OS) (6 Questions)
- **OS Architecture:** Functions, Batch, Multitasking, Time-sharing, Multiprocessing, RTOS.
- **Interface & Terms:** GUI vs CLI, Kernel, Shell, User mode vs Kernel mode, system calls, cold/warm boot, BIOS, UEFI, POST.
- **File & Memory:** Files, directories, paths, permissions, virtual memory, paging & segmentation.
- **Process & Device:** Process states, FCFS, SJF, Priority, Round Robin, drivers, interrupts, spooling, buffering.

### E. MS Office & Database (9 Questions)
- **MS Word:** Formatting, margins, headers/footers, tables, Find & Replace, Mail Merge, section breaks, DOCX, keyboard shortcuts.
- **MS Excel:** Worksheets, cells, relative/absolute references, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, IF, COUNTIF, charts, PivotTables, error codes.
- **MS PowerPoint:** Slides, Slide Master, layouts, transitions vs animations, presenter view, PPTX, shortcuts.
- **MS Access & DB:** DBMS/RDBMS, tables, fields, records, primary/foreign/candidate keys, relationships, queries, basic SQL, ACCDB.

### F. Networking & Data Communication (22 Questions)
- **Network Fundamentals:** LAN, MAN, WAN, PAN, peer-to-peer, client-server, topologies (bus, star, ring, mesh, tree, hybrid).
- **Network Devices:** NIC, Hub, Switch, Router, Modem, Repeater, Bridge, Gateway, Access Point, Firewall.
- **Transmission:** Simplex, half-duplex, full-duplex, twisted pair, coaxial, optical fibre, wireless media, bandwidth.
- **Protocols & Models:** OSI 7 Layers, TCP/IP Model, TCP vs UDP, HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SFTP, SMTP, POP3, IMAP, DNS, DHCP.
- **Addressing:** IPv4 vs IPv6, public vs private IP, static vs dynamic, MAC address, port numbers, URLs, domain name system.
- **Internet & Web:** WWW, web browsers, search engines, cookies, cache, email headers, cloud computing (IaaS/PaaS/SaaS), VoIP.

### G. Cyber Security & IT Law (6 Questions)
- **Security Basics:** CIA triad, authentication, authorization, MFA, encryption, digital signatures, backup types.
- **Threats & Malware:** Virus, worm, Trojan, spyware, ransomware, keylogger, phishing, spoofing, social engineering, DoS/DDoS.
- **Law & Safety:** Safe password practices, Wi-Fi safety, copyright, IPR, IT Act 2000 provisions & cybercrime awareness.

### H. Programming, Algorithms & IDE (26 Questions)
- **Programming Basics:** Compilers, interpreters, assemblers, source/object code, variables, data types, operators, precedence.
- **C Language:** Structure of C, tokens, data types, printf/scanf, conditionals, loops, arrays, strings, functions, recursion, pointers basics.
- **Algorithms:** Definition, characteristics, sequence, selection, iteration, dry run, trace table, time complexity basics.
- **Flowcharts:** Terminal, process, input/output, decision, connectors, arrows, pseudocode conventions.
- **Searching & Sorting:** Linear search, binary search, bubble sort, selection sort, insertion sort.
- **OOP Concepts:** Class, object, encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance, polymorphism, overloading vs overriding, constructors.
- **IDE Tools:** Features, code completion, debugging, breakpoints, NetBeans features & benefits.

## 3. Subject 2: Reasoning & Mental Ability (30 Questions – 20%)

1. **Number & Alphabet Series (3 Questions):** Number series, letter series, missing/wrong term, alpha-numeric series.
2. **Coding-Decoding (3 Questions):** Letter shifting, reverse alphabet, number coding, substitution, conditional coding.
3. **Blood Relations (2 Questions):** Family relations, family tree diagrams, coded relations, pointing-to-person.
4. **Direction Sense (1 Question):** Direction turns, distance, shortest distance, shadow questions.
5. **Analogy (2 Questions):** Number, letter, word, relationship & classification analogy.
6. **Classification / Odd One Out (2 Questions):** Number, letter, word, figure classification, odd pair.
7. **Alphabetical Order (1 Question):** Dictionary order, word arrangement, alphabet test.
8. **Mathematical Operations (1 Question):** Symbol replacement, sign interchanging, equation balancing.
9. **Data Sufficiency (4 Questions):** Two and three statement sufficiency.
10. **Arrangement & Puzzles (3 Questions):** Linear/circular seating arrangement, ranking, scheduling, floor puzzles.
11. **Logical Reasoning (4 Questions):** Statements & conclusions, assumptions, course of action, syllogism, Venn diagrams.
12. **Non-Verbal Reasoning (4 Questions):** Mirror/water images, figure completion, paper folding, counting figures.

## 4. Subject 3: Mathematics / Quantitative Aptitude (20 Questions – 13.3%)

1. **Algebra & Word Problems (3 Questions):** Linear equations, algebraic identities, age problems, number equations.
2. **HCF, LCM & Divisibility (3 Questions):** Prime factorization, HCF/LCM relations, word problems, divisibility rules.
3. **Simplification & Decimals (3 Questions):** BODMAS rule, fractions, decimal operations, approximation.
4. **Percentage (1 Question):** Percentage basics, fraction-to-percentage, increase/decrease, successive changes.
5. **Average (1 Question):** Average basics, missing values, average age, combined average.
6. **Permutation & Combination (1 Question):** Fundamental counting principle, factorials, handshake problems, arrangements.
7. **Data Interpretation (DI) (8 Questions):** Tables, bar graphs, pie charts, line graphs, percentage/ratio analysis from data.
8. **Number System:** Natural, prime, composite numbers, unit digits, squares, cubes, remainders.

   - Polity & Administration: Governor, CM, Legislative Assembly, High Court, Lokayukta, Panchayati Raj.
   - Economy & Schemes: Budget highlights, state government schemes, development programmes.
2. **Indian Geography & Census (2 Questions):** Physical divisions, rivers, wildlife, census literacy & sex ratio.
3. **Sports & Current Affairs (2 Questions):** Olympics, Asian Games, awards, appointments, major IT developments.
4. **General Science (1 Question):** Human body, organ systems, human eye, vitamins, diseases, everyday science.
